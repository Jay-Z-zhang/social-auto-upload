Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Social Auto-Publish ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a content scheduling and publishing platform that helps creators distribute video content to social media platforms including TikTok and YouTube. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may collect:

  • Account Information: When you connect your TikTok or YouTube account, we receive an OAuth access token that allows us to publish content on your behalf. We do not receive or store your platform passwords.
  • Video Content: Videos you upload to our Service for publishing to connected platforms.
  • Metadata: Titles, descriptions, tags, and scheduling preferences you provide for your videos.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the Service, including publish history and account activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Publish video content to your connected social media accounts as you direct
  • Schedule posts according to your preferences
  • Display publishing status and history
  • Improve and maintain the Service

3. Data Storage and Security

Your OAuth tokens and video files are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a centralized server that stores your personal data or video content. When you initiate a publish action, your content is transmitted directly to the target platform (TikTok, YouTube, etc.) using their official APIs.

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of OAuth tokens.

4. Third-Party Services

Our Service integrates with third-party platforms including:

  • TikTok: For video publishing via the TikTok Content Posting API
  • YouTube: For video publishing via the YouTube Data API

Your use of these platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We only share data with connected platforms (TikTok, YouTube) as necessary to perform the publishing services you request.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Disconnect accounts: Revoke access to any connected platform at any time
  • Delete data: Remove your account and associated data from the Service
  • Access data: Request a copy of your data stored by the Service

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Service. When you disconnect an account or delete your data, we remove the associated OAuth tokens and metadata from local storage.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
