1. Introduction

Social Auto-Publish ("we," "our," "us," or the "Service") is a content scheduling and publishing platform that enables content creators to distribute video content to social media platforms including TikTok, YouTube, and others.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our Service. By accessing or using Social Auto-Publish, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. This policy is designed to comply with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA)
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents
  • The UK Data Protection Act 2018 for users in the United Kingdom
  • Other applicable regional and national privacy laws

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Category Examples Purpose
Account Information Username, email address Account creation and authentication
OAuth Credentials Access tokens from TikTok, YouTube Publishing content on your behalf
Content Data Videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails Publishing to connected platforms
Scheduling Preferences Publish times, frequency settings Automated scheduling functionality

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service, including features used, actions taken, and timestamps
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, referring URLs
  • Publishing History: Records of content published through the Service, including status and outcomes

2.3 Information from Third Parties

When you connect your social media accounts, we receive limited information from those platforms as permitted by their APIs:

  • TikTok: User ID, username, profile information as permitted by TikTok's API scopes
  • YouTube: Channel ID, channel name, as permitted by YouTube Data API

We do not receive or store your passwords for any connected platforms.

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Local Storage Architecture

Social Auto-Publish is designed with privacy in mind:

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device
  • Video files remain on your local machine until you initiate upload
  • We do not operate centralized servers that store your personal content

6.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of OAuth tokens at rest
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all API communications
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel

6.3 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and relevant supervisory authorities as required by applicable law, typically within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax, accounting requirements)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

When you disconnect an account or request deletion:

  • OAuth tokens are immediately removed from local storage
  • Publishing history may be retained for up to 30 days for operational purposes
  • Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for analytics

To exercise your rights, please contact us at jayz_z@foxmail.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.
